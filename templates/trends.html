📐 Explainable Mathematical Formulation (Viva Defense Ready)
Growth = (Recent - Previous) / max(Previous, 1)
Emerging Flag = (Recent ≥ 3) ∧ (Growth ≥ +50%)
Auditing Logic: The agent aggregates extracted trend labels and named entities. It partitions article publication dates into two distinct non-overlapping 7-day temporal windows: $t_{0}$ to $t_{-7}$ (Recent) versus $t_{-7}$ to $t_{-14}$ (Previous). Trends with accelerating mention volume exceeding 50% relative surge are escalated as Emerging Signals.
